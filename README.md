NEON PALMS — a Sunfall City story is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in a desktop browser. It is written in vanilla JavaScript with Canvas 2D — no libraries, no build step, and no external assets; every street, sound, and sprite is generated by the code in this repository. You roam Sunfall City, a sun-soaked coastal city of six districts, on foot and by car, and any vehicle you can reach is one you can steal and drive. Crime draws heat: a five-star wanted system brings police pursuits, pinning maneuvers, and roadblocks down on you, and getting busted or wasted costs you cash and your freedom. Between escapes you work for fixer Delia Vance through a six-mission story chain, and pick up taxi fares and timed courier runs as side jobs to build your bankroll. A circular minimap HUD tracks the city, the law, and your next waypoint, progress is kept in local saves, and the engine hum, sirens, and gunfire are all synthesized live with WebAudio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Controls: WASD/arrows move and drive, F enter/leave car, E interact and take jobs, mouse aim and click to shoot on foot, Shift run, Space handbrake, M mute, Esc pause.
