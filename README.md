NEON PALMS — a Sunfall City story. An original open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from static files, written in vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D with no libraries and no assets: everything is drawn and synthesized in code. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city of six districts on foot and by car, steal and drive any vehicle, dodge or fight a five-star wanted system with police pursuits and roadblocks, work a six-mission story chain for fixer Delia Vance plus taxi and courier side jobs, and keep your progress in a local save. Full HUD with a circular minimap, and a WebAudio-synthesized engine, sirens and score.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Controls: WASD / arrows to move and drive, F to enter or leave a car, E to interact and take jobs, mouse to aim and click to shoot on foot, Shift to run, Space for the handbrake, M to mute, Esc to pause.
