NEON PALMS — a Sunfall City story is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in a desktop browser, hand-built in vanilla JavaScript on Canvas 2D with no libraries, no frameworks, and no external assets — every sprite, sound, and street is generated in code. You roam Sunfall City, a sun-drenched coastal city of six distinct districts, on foot or behind the wheel, and any vehicle you can reach is yours to steal and drive. Trouble scales with a five-star wanted system: commit crimes and the police answer with escalating pursuits, spike-fast interceptors, and roadblocks sealing off the avenues. A six-mission story chain for fixer Delia Vance threads through the city, with taxi fares and courier runs as side jobs whenever you need cash. A circular minimap HUD keeps the action readable at speed, progress persists in local saves, and the whole soundscape — engines, sirens, and a synthwave score — is synthesized live with WebAudio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Controls: WASD/arrows move and drive, F enter/leave car, E interact and take jobs, mouse aim and click to shoot on foot, Shift run, Space handbrake, M mute, Esc pause.
