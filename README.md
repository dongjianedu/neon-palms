NEON PALMS — a Sunfall City story is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in a desktop browser. It is written in vanilla JavaScript on Canvas 2D with no libraries, no build step and no downloaded assets: every street, car, palm tree and sound is drawn or synthesized by the code itself. Sunfall City is a sun-soaked coastal city of six districts — Downtown, Palm Beach, Portside, the Old Market, Hillside Heights and the Marina — wrapped around a bay, with live traffic, pedestrians, shops and landmarks to explore on foot or behind the wheel. Any vehicle you can reach is yours to steal, from parked coupes to taxis mid-route, but crime draws heat: a five-star wanted system brings Harbor PD cruisers after you with intercept driving, roadblocks and a bust meter if they pin you down. Between chases you work for fixer Delia Vance through a six-mission story chain — pickups, deliveries, a borrowed car that must come back clean, and a finale that asks you to outrun the whole department — while SunCab taxi fares and timed courier runs keep cash coming in on the side. A circular minimap HUD tracks your position, police blips and job waypoints, progress is kept in a local save, and the engine hum, sirens, gunfire and music are all synthesized live with WebAudio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Controls: WASD/arrows move and drive, F enter/leave car, E interact and take jobs, mouse aim and click to shoot on foot, Shift run, Space handbrake, M mute, Esc pause.
